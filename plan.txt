### 🎯 Problem Statement

Colleges often have students whose academic performance gradually decreases. Teachers may not identify students who are at risk until their results become poor.

Build a web application that uses **Machine Learning to predict a student's performance/risk level** based on factors such as:

* Attendance %
* Previous semester marks
* Internal assessment marks
* Assignment completion
* Study hours
* Previous backlogs
* Number of failed subjects
* Participation/activities
* Previous semester GPA

The system predicts something like:

> 🟢 **Low Risk**
> 🟡 **Moderate Risk**
> 🔴 **High Risk**
